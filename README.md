# 📺 YouTube Trending Video Analysis – Mid-term BI Project [Dashboard_link](https://public.tableau.com/shared/832FC9D9P?:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

A collaborative data analysis project exploring what makes a YouTube video go viral. Using a combination of **Python, SQL, and Tableau**, we analyzed trending video statistics to uncover patterns in viewer engagement, content popularity, and creator behavior.

## 🎯 Objective

> _Identify factors and patterns that make a YouTube video trend on the platform._

This project leverages trending video data to analyze:
- Viewer interaction metrics
- Content trends
- Channel behavior
- Keywords and engagement drivers

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Overview

- 📦 Source: YouTube Trending Video Statistics (India)  
- 🕒 Daily snapshots of top-trending videos  
- 📁 Key fields: Title, Channel, Views, Likes, Dislikes, Comments, Tags, Publish Time, Trending Date, Category

---

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies Used

- 🐍 **Python** – Data cleaning (Pandas), EDA, and word cloud generation  
- 🗃️ **SQL** – Querying the cleaned data for insights  
- 📊 **Tableau** – Interactive dashboards and data visualization  
- 📎 **Google Colab** / Jupyter Notebook – Python execution and collaboration

---

## 🗓️ Project Timeline

### **Day 1** – Data Cleaning & EDA
- Removed nulls and irrelevant columns  
- Parsed and standardized timestamps  
- Initial exploration of views, likes, and comment distribution  

### **Day 2** – Advanced Analysis
- 📈 Measured interaction metrics: views, likes, comments, dislikes  
- 🔖 Analyzed popular tags & most frequent keywords (via Python word cloud)  
- 📊 Identified top 10 most liked, disliked, commented, and viewed videos  
- 🕒 Calculated "trending temperature" (time to trend after upload)  
- 😄 Identified positive vs. negative reaction videos  
- 🧓 Measured video age vs. performance  
- 📺 Compared performance against recent uploads from the same channel

### **Day 3** – SQL & Tableau
- Finalized SQL queries for segmenting, ranking, and calculating engagement ratios  
- Designed Tableau dashboard with filters and interactive charts

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 🎶 **Music** and **Entertainment** dominate trending categories  
- 🏆 A few channels consistently appear in trending, showing content strategy strength  
- 💬 Videos with more comments often indicate higher engagement  
- ⏳ Videos that trended within 24–48 hours of upload performed best  
- 🔥 Certain keywords and tags (e.g., “official,” “trailer,” “new song”) are common in trending titles  
- 😐 Some of the most disliked videos also ranked among the most viewed, indicating controversial virality
